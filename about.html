Australia First Foundation

The Australia First Foundation is a grassroots, youth-focused movement dedicated to empowering the next generation of Australian leaders. Established to bridge the gap between traditional values and modern civic engagement, the foundation provides education, robust open forums, and leadership development programs. We are committed to fostering deep national pride, defending individual liberties, and providing a powerful, united platform for young conservative voices across the nation to actively shape public policy.

Governance & Transparency

The Australia First Foundation states that it operates under a framework of transparency, financial accountability, and public disclosure. Its executive leadership team is described as consisting of civic organizers, community leaders, and policy advisors responsible for organizational activities under its non-profit charter.

Electoral Authorisation & Compliance

Australian electoral authorisation requirements depend on the nature of the communication and the entity responsible for it. The Foundation should ensure each campaign, digital asset and printed item carries any authorisation and disclosure particulars required by applicable Commonwealth and state law. Media, disclosure and privacy enquiries can be directed through the Foundation’s central contact channels.
