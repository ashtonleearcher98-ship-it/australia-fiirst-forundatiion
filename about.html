Australia First Foundation

The Australia First Foundation is a grassroots, youth-focused movement dedicated to empowering the next generation of Australian leaders. Established to bridge the gap between traditional values and modern civic engagement, the foundation provides education, robust open forums, and leadership development programs. We are committed to fostering deep national pride, defending individual liberties, and providing a powerful, united platform for young conservative voices across the nation to actively shape public policy.

Governance & Transparency

The Australia First Foundation operates under a strict framework of transparency, financial accountability, and commitment to public disclosure. Our executive leadership team consists of dedicated civic organizers, community leaders, and policy advisors who ensure all organizational activities align seamlessly with our core non-profit charter.

Electoral Authorisation & Compliance

In accordance with applicable Australian electoral laws and state-level funding regulations, campaign and promotional material should carry the authorisation and disclosure particulars required for the relevant communication and responsible entity. For media inquiries, official disclosure statements, or organizational privacy information, contact the administrative compliance office through the Foundation’s central digital channels.
