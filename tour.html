Australia Rising Tour 2026

The Australia Rising Tour is the premier youth-led movement traveling across the nation to spark honest discussions, challenge conventions, and empower a new generation of leaders. Moving dynamically through regional hubs and major cities, this tour brings vital civic engagement straight to your doorstep. Explore our active roadmap below to see when the tour is flying into your local region.

Tour format

What to Expect at Every Stop

Live debates, interactive Q&A sessions, and open forums.

Our tour format is built to prioritize active audience participation over static lectures. Every single stop features:

01

Live Debates

Hard-hitting, unscripted panel sessions tackling economics, limited government, and cultural preservation.

02

Interactive Q&A

Open floor time where you get to directly question panels, challenge ideas, and voice local regional concerns.

03

Open Forums

Grassroots social spaces to network, collaborate, and build strong community alliances with like-minded young patriots.

Confirmed scheduling and specific venue information will be published here dynamically as bookings are locked in.
