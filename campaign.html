Foundation position

Foundation position

The Australia First Foundation states that it is committed to defending what it describes as the fundamental right to life for the most vulnerable in society. Its core campaign objective is to advocate for legislative reform across the nation, focusing heavily on its primary “Ban Abortion 2026” initiative. The Foundation says it aims to shift public discourse and educate younger Australians on pro-life principles through youth outreach, community distribution of educational materials, and digital advocacy. Its stated objective is to build support for legal protections and traditional moral frameworks.

02
Reference material

Legal & factual references

Abortion access in Australia is governed primarily by state and territory law. In New South Wales, the Abortion Law Reform Act 2019 provides for termination by prescribed health practitioners at not more than 22 weeks and sets separate requirements for termination after 22 weeks. The Foundation states that it uses these legislative frameworks to inform its advocacy and public discussion.

Primary reference: NSW Abortion Law Reform Act 2019. Legal material should be checked against the current legislation before publication.
