Foundation position

Foundation position

The Australia First Foundation states that it is committed to defending what it describes as the fundamental right to life for vulnerable members of society. Its core campaign objective is to advocate for legislative reform through its “Ban Abortion 2026” initiative. The Foundation says the campaign will focus on youth outreach, distribution of educational materials, community activity and digital advocacy, with the stated aim of promoting pro-life principles and traditional moral frameworks.

Reference material

Legal & factual references

Abortion law is primarily regulated by Australia’s states and territories. In New South Wales, the Abortion Law Reform Act 2019 provides for termination by prescribed health practitioners at not more than 22 weeks and sets separate requirements after 22 weeks. The Foundation uses state legislative frameworks as reference material for its policy advocacy and public discussion.

Primary reference: NSW Abortion Law Reform Act 2019. Check the current legislation before publishing legal claims.
